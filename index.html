Questions

Before you ask.

How fast is "two minutes," really?

The wizard takes about two minutes: Instagram handle, email, theme. The build itself usually finishes in under a minute, and a login link lands in your inbox when it's done.

Is the free plan actually free forever?

Yes. Free gets you the full five-page site, booking forms, Instagram sync, and five content edits a month — no credit card, no trial clock. Pro removes the edit limit and adds your own domain.

What do you build the site from?

Your public Instagram profile: name, bio, and recent work. We never ask for your password, and the copy stays grounded in what's actually on your profile.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes — the dashboard has a click-to-edit visual editor. Change text, swap the hero, reorder or add gallery photos, switch colors and fonts. No code anywhere.

Can I use my own domain?

On Pro, yes — connect any domain you own and we handle the rest. Free sites live at eternowebstudio.com/yourname.
