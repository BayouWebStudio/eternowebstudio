Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. Enter your Instagram handle and we build everything. You edit your site from a visual dashboard — no code ever.

How long does it take to build my site?

About 2-4 minutes. Our system scrapes your Instagram, classifies your style, and deploys a 5-page site automatically.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes — on the Pro plan you can connect any custom domain (.com, .ink, .art, etc.) or purchase one directly from your dashboard.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime from your dashboard. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Your free site stays live on our subdomain.

What does the AI booking agent do?

It's a phone-based AI assistant that answers calls, books appointments, and takes messages when you're busy tattooing.

Can I see examples of sites you've built?

Absolutely — check out our portfolio to see 17+ live sites.
