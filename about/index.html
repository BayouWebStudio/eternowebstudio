About Eterno

A craft studio for artists who want their work online — without the hassle.

Our Story

Built by people who get it

Eterno was founded in Houston, TX and Mexico City by a team that saw the same problem everywhere: tattoo artists needed websites, but hated the process of building them. Drag-and-drop builders felt generic. Agencies were expensive and slow. Most artists gave up and stayed on Instagram alone.

We built Eterno to change that. We automate the entire technical side — pulling your portfolio straight from Instagram, handling hosting, domains, SEO, and updates — so you never have to think about code or configurations. You focus on your craft. We handle the rest.

Today the team personally builds and manages 17+ client sites, treating every one like our own. No outsourcing, no templates. Just careful work from people who care about the details.

Studio Philosophy
We are not a website builder. We are not a tech platform. We are a craft studio — like a tattoo shop, but for websites.

Craft Over Templates

Every site is built from your actual Instagram — not a generic template. Your portfolio, your style, your identity.

Zero Tech Required

You never touch code, DNS, or hosting. We handle everything so you can stay focused on your art.

Built to Last

Your site runs forever. No lock-in, no surprise fees. Grandfathered rates for every client, always.
