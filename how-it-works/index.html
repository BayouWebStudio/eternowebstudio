The Process

How It Works

From Instagram to live website in 4 easy steps. No tech knowledge required.

Step by Step

01

Enter Your Instagram

We scrape your photos, bio, services, and contact info automatically. Just enter your handle — we do the rest.

02

Pick Your Style

Choose from 6 hand-crafted themes designed for creative professionals: Geometric, Neo Traditional, Blackwork, Realism, New School, and Watercolor. Or let us auto-detect the perfect match from your photos.

03

Choose Your Layout

Select how your site is structured: Monolith (single column), Split (side-by-side), Showcase (hero + grid), or Raw (dense magazine). Can't decide? Pick "Surprise Me" and we'll choose for you.

04

Sign Up & Build

Create a free account and your site starts building immediately. In 2–4 minutes you'll have a live 5-page website: Home, About, Gallery, Booking, and Contact.

Questions

Frequently Asked

How much does a tattoo artist website cost?
Eterno offers a free plan with a custom 5-page website and free subdomain. The Pro plan is $25/month and includes a custom domain, analytics, AI booking agent, and priority support. No setup fees, no contracts.
Do I need to know how to code?
Not at all. Enter your Instagram handle and we build everything. You edit your site from a visual dashboard — no code, no DNS, no hosting required.
How long does it take to build my site?
About 2-4 minutes. Our system scrapes your Instagram, classifies your style, and deploys a 5-page site (Home, About, Gallery, Booking, Contact) automatically.
Can I use my own domain?
Yes — on the Pro plan you can connect any custom domain (.com, .ink, .art, etc.) or purchase one directly from your dashboard.
Can I turn my Instagram into a website?
That's exactly what Eterno does. Enter your handle and we scrape your photos, bio, and services to build a professional site. Your gallery stays in sync with your latest posts.
What does the AI booking agent do?
It's a phone-based AI assistant that answers calls, books appointments, and takes messages when you're busy tattooing. Included in the Pro plan.
What if I want to cancel?
Cancel anytime from your dashboard. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Your free site stays live on our subdomain even if you downgrade.
