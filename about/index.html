About Eterno

A craft studio for artists who want their work online — without the hassle.

Our Story

Built by people who get it

Eterno was founded in Houston, TX by a team that saw the same problem everywhere: tattoo artists needed websites, but hated the process of building them. Drag-and-drop builders felt generic. Agencies were expensive and slow. Most artists gave up and stayed on Instagram alone.

We built Eterno to change that. The platform automates the entire technical side — pulling your portfolio straight from Instagram, handling hosting, domains, SEO, and updates — so you never touch code or configurations. You focus on your craft. Eterno handles the rest.

Today 17+ artists use Eterno to run their online presence. Each site starts from their Instagram and one of 6 artist-designed themes — then they customize everything from a visual dashboard. No coding, no waiting on a developer.

Studio Philosophy
We are not a generic website builder. We are not a drag-and-drop page editor. We are a creative platform — built by artists, for artists.

Your Style, Your Site

Pick from 6 artist-designed themes and 4 layouts, then customize everything. Built from your actual Instagram — your portfolio, your style, your identity.

Zero Tech Required

You never touch code, DNS, or hosting. The platform handles everything so you can stay focused on your art.

Built to Last

Your site runs forever. No lock-in, no surprise fees. Grandfathered rates for every client, always.
